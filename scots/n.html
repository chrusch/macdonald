Scots Words: N

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWYZ

na

no; not (in negative verbs: canna, dinna)

702 in all 11 novels. Forms: na (652), 'na (49), na' (1). One of the 100 commonest.

The minister was na fit to pray, And lat alane to preach Alec Forbes of Howglen

nae

no, not any

715 in all 11 novels. Forms: nae (704), 'nae (11). One of the 100 commonest.

We think to hear nae mair o' him, Till we come hame at nicht Alec Forbes of Howglen

naebody

nobody

154 in 9 novels. Forms: naebody (144), naebody's (7), 'naebody (3).

Naebody had ony business to tell ye ony sic a thing,” returned Aggie, rather angrily. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

naegait

nowhere; in no way

7 in 5 novels. Forms: naegait (6), naegate (1).

Weel ken I ye’ll tak him naegait but whaur it’s weel he sud gang! Heather and Snow

naething

nothing

542 in all 11 novels. Forms: naething (536), naething's (3), noathing (1), naethin' (1), noething (1). One of the 100 commonest.

I ken naething aboot yer muckle or yer little Ms," retorted Tibbie, with indignation. Alec Forbes of Howglen

naewise

in no way

3 in David Elginbrod and Heather and Snow. Forms: naewise (3).

Here’s you and me stanin murnin ower anither man’s bairn, and naewise kennin what’s come o’ oor ain twa! Heather and Snow

naig

nag, horse

5 in Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: naig (5).

Catch yer naig an’ pu’ his tail: In his hin’ heel ca’ a nail Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

nain

4 in 4 novels. Forms: nain (4).

Sleep intil the efternune o’ the neist day he wad, but never oot o’ ‘s nain bed Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

8 in Alec Forbes of Howglen and Malcolm. Forms: nainsel (7), nainsel' (1).

Ye s' hae as muckle o' mine to yer nainsel' as'll clear Mrs Forbes," said Annie. Alec Forbes of Howglen

naisty

nasty

4 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: naisty (4).

He's a naisty mean cratur. Alec Forbes of Howglen

naither

neither

63 in 8 novels. Forms: naither (53), nowther (5), neyther (3), nouther (2).

But that’s naither here nor there, an’ has naething to du wi’ onybody ava’. Malcolm

13 in 7 novels. Forms: nait'ral (5), naitral (4), naiteral (2), nateral (2).

Gien she war my ain mither, there bude to be some nait’ral drawin’s atween ‘s, a body wad think. Malcolm

I’m mair likly to get the bruised reed intil my nakit loof (palm)! Salted with Fire

ye never luik an inch afore the pint o' yer ain neb. Alec Forbes of Howglen

’At yer neebour’s jist whaever lies neist ye i’ need o’ yer help. Heather and Snow

necessity

4 in Sir Gibbie and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: needcessity (3), needcessities (1).

We maunna be sairer upo’ him, nor the needcessity laid upo’ his. Sir Gibbie

needfu'

needful; needy

But wha’ll tak the trible needfu’ to the learnin’ o’ a puir dummie? Sir Gibbie

needed

3 in Heather and Snow, Malcolm and Salted with Fire. Forms: needit (3).

She needit a heap o’ luikin efter, as ye said. Heather and Snow

46 in 9 novels. Forms: needna (45), needsna (1).

But he never said it, and ye needna try to threpe it upo’ me! Heather and Snow

Some fowk likes the taste o' neeps, though I dinna like it mysel'," answered Bruce. Alec Forbes of Howglen

I houp sair they’ll hae something for auld fowk to du i’ the neist. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

There’s some fowk ‘at can respeck no airgument but frae steekit neives David Elginbrod

I houp nane o’ them’s swallowed my nepkin! Sir Gibbie
