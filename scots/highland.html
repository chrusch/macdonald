Highland English

Duncan MacPhail in Malcolm and The Marquis of Lossie, and a few other Gaelic speakers in the novels, talk not Scots but the English of a Highlander whose first language is Gaelic. MacDonald marks it chiefly by unvoicing (b to p, d to t, g to c or ch, v to f), by she for every person (she’ll pe telling you for “I’ll tell you”), and by ta for “the”. These words are not underlined in the novels. The spellings below are those found in them; they are not the whole of it.
