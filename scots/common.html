A key to the spelling

Much of MacDonald’s Scots is English words in Scots sounds, spelt as said. Once these patterns are familiar, most of them read at sight.

Scots English Examples
oo ou, ow oot out, hoose house, doon down, noo now, toon town
ui, u oo, o guid good, luik look, buik book, puir poor, sune soon, fule fool
ei, ee ea, e, i heid head, deid dead, breid bread, deevil devil, speerit spirit
