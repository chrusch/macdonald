Scots Words

Scots Words: Q

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWYZ

quaiet

quiet

100 in 10 novels. Forms: quaiet (80), quaietly (7), quaieter (4), quaietest (3), quaietness (3), quaietit (1), quaiets (1), quaitet (1).

and it was no to be expecket he cud be sae quaiet as anither. Alec Forbes of Howglen

quawlifee

qualify

1 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: quawlifee (1).

It tak's a hantle to quawlifee sic weet's this. Alec Forbes of Howglen

quean

young woman; a hussy

11 in 6 novels. Forms: quean (8), queans (1), quean's (1), queyn (1).

'Shargar may be a quean efter a', for onything 'at I ken to the contrairy Robert Falconer

queentry

country

4 in Malcolm. Forms: queentry (4).

Tell me ‘at ye dinna ken what a’ the queentry kens Malcolm

queets

ankles

2 in Heather and Snow. Forms: queets (2).

MacDonald glosses it: “ankles”.

I wudna care sae muckle gien he didna grip me by the queets (ankles), like! Heather and Snow

queston

question

54 in 9 novels. Forms: queston (37), questons (11), queston's (2), quest'ons (2), questoned (1), quest'on (1).

But there’s something o’ mair importance yet nor kennin’ the richt o’ ony queston. Donal Grant

quha

who

4 in Malcolm. Forms: quha (4).

The lord quha wad sup on 3 thowmes o’ cauld airn, The ayr quha wad kythe a bastard and carena, The mayd quha wad tyne … Malcolm

quhilk

which

1 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: quhilk (1).

And I tried to gie birth till a sang, the quhilk, like Jove, I conceived i' my heid last nicht. Alec Forbes of Howglen

quittance

discharge, riddance

4 in Alec Forbes of Howglen and Malcolm. Forms: quittance (4).

Gin ye gie me a quittance for a hunner an' fifty poun', I'll gie her a receipt. Alec Forbes of Howglen

quo'

quoth, said

17 in 6 novels. Forms: quo' (17).

but a’ was peace an’ hermony, as quo’ the auld sang aboot bonnie Kilmeny Donal Grant
