Scots Words

Scots Words: J

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWYZ

jabble

ripple, choppy water

4 in Alec Forbes of Howglen and Sir Gibbie. Forms: jabble (4).

Whan ye jabble a stank, the stink rises. Alec Forbes of Howglen

jaloose

guess, suspect

8 in 5 novels. Forms: jaloosin' (3), jaloosin (2), jaloose (2), jaloosed (1).

I’ the meantime, hooever, we canna gi’e in till ‘t, ’cause it wad set fowk jaloosin’. The Marquis of Lossie

jaud

jade, hussy

9 in 5 novels. Forms: jaud (7), jawds (1), jaud'll (1).

I dinna ken what gars me keep the jaud. The Marquis of Lossie

jaup

splash

2 in Malcolm and Robert Falconer. Forms: jaups (1), jaup (1).

The door's lockit, an' Jeames Jaup, they tell me 's tane awa' the key. Robert Falconer

jaw

wave, billow; talk, cheek

10 in 5 novels. Forms: jaws (5), jawin' (5).

MacDonald glosses it: “waves”; “billows”.

The jaws (waves) gang doon as gin they war sae mony wholpies! Alec Forbes of Howglen

jawbane

jawbone

1 in Malcolm. Forms: jawbane (1).

… speech o’ ‘im, for whan he’s frichtit he can hardly muv is jawbane Malcolm

jeally

jelly

1 in Donal Grant. Forms: jeally (1).

I’m sorry I hae nae jeally or jam to set afore ye, sir,” said Doory, “we’re but semple fowk, ye see Donal Grant

Jeames

James

97 in 7 novels. Forms: jeames (97).

Jeames, ye're takin' the pairt o' Sawton upo' ye, drivin' a man frae his prayers! Alec Forbes of Howglen

jeedge

judge

27 in 8 novels. Forms: jeedge (22), jeedgin' (2), jeedges (1), jeedged (1), jeedgin (1).

ane, that is, wha’ll jeedge ye truly and mercifully, and no condemn ye affhan’. Salted with Fire

jeedgment

judgement

33 in 8 novels. Forms: jeedgment (26), jeedgement (6), jeedgement's (1).

an’ some thinks he’s doomed to haunt the hoose till the day o’ jeedgment. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

jeist

jest

8 in Alec Forbes of Howglen and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: jeist (5), jeistin' (2), jeists (1).

Gien they binna ordeent o’ God, what are they but a jeist? Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

jiffie

moment

12 in 5 novels. Forms: jiffey (8), jiffie (4).

MacDonald glosses it: “moment”.

an’ the lads ’ll be out to leuk for her in a jiffey. David Elginbrod

jine

join

14 in 5 novels. Forms: jined (7), jine (5), jinin' (1), jines (1).

I some think it has been ance disconneckit frae the lan’, an’ jined on by a draw-brig. Malcolm

jink

dodge

10 in Heather and Snow, Sir Gibbie and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: jink (9), jinkit (1).

To gie yer siller to ither fowk to spread is to jink the wark laid oot for ye. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

jints

joints

2 in Malcolm and Sir Gibbie. Forms: jints (2).

he wad hae dune ‘t in half the time, an’ no raxed his jints. Sir Gibbie

jist

just

1,057 in all 11 novels. Forms: jist (1,051), 'jist (6). One of the 100 commonest.

but jist say a fair word for the livin' ower the deid, ye ken. Alec Forbes of Howglen

j'ists

joists

1 in Sir Gibbie. Forms: j'ists (1).

Gien the j’ists be strang, an’ weel set intil the wa’s, what for sudna ye tak the horse up the stair intil yer bedrooms? Sir Gibbie

jobby

little job

1 in Heather and Snow. Forms: jobby (1).

I hae jist the last stroke to gie this bit jobby. Heather and Snow

jography

geography

1 in Sir Gibbie. Forms: jography (1).

But may be my jography buik’s some auld-fashioned. Sir Gibbie

jooggy

jug, a dram

1 in Sir Gibbie. Forms: jooggy (1).

… hae prayt a word to yer lordship gien I hadna had a jooggy or twa first. Sir Gibbie

jouk

duck, dodge

1 in Malcolm. Forms: joukit (1).

MacDonald glosses it: “without ducking”.

Lat ye the jaw (wave) gae ower ohn joukit (without ducking). Malcolm

jummle

jumble

2 in Alec Forbes of Howglen and Malcolm. Forms: jummlin' (1), jummle (1).

whan their buiks gang wrang ye ken, for figures is some ill for jummlin'. Alec Forbes of Howglen

jumps

tallies, agrees

3* in Alec Forbes of Howglen, Malcolm and Robert Falconer. Forms: jumps (3).

But wi’ that up jumps Bawby, an’ comin’ efter her, says to me Malcolm

justifee

justify

8 in 6 novels. Forms: justifee (4), justifeed (2), justifeein' (2).

I haena the licht to justifee me in denyin’ ‘t. Donal Grant

jyler

jailer

1 in Robert Falconer. Forms: jyler (1).

by a jyler that winna tyne the grup.' Robert Falconer
