Scots Words

Scots Words: V

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWYZ

vaigabon'

vagabond

3 in Alec Forbes of Howglen and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: vaigabon' (1), vaigabone (1), vaggabon (1).

She'll be awa' wi' that vaigabone, Willie Macwha. Alec Forbes of Howglen

vailue

value

5 in Malcolm, Robert Falconer and Sir Gibbie. Forms: vailue (5).

There was ane thoucht them o’ vailue,” replied Miss Horn Malcolm

vainish

vanish

4 in 4 novels. Forms: vainished (2), vainishes (1), vainishin' (1).

they're vainishin' even noo frae the earth, though it doesna luik like it in Rag-fair or Petticoat-lane. Robert Falconer

vainity

vanity

4 in Malcolm, Robert Falconer and Sir Gibbie. Forms: vainities (2), vainity (2).

What comes o’ yer seemile than, anent the vainity o’ their endeevour? Sir Gibbie

varily

verily

1 in Donal Grant. Forms: varily (1).

An’ I varily believe she’s safer there wi’ you an’ the hoosekeeper nor whaur he could win at her easier, an’ whaur they wud be … Donal Grant

varra, verra

very

360 in all 11 novels. Forms: verra (291), vera (46), varra (21), 'verra (2). One of the 100 commonest.

I'm verra sorry, Maister Malison, to come to you wi' my complaints," said Bruce Alec Forbes of Howglen

veesible

visible

1 in David Elginbrod. Forms: veesible (1).

… the sicht o’ them ‘at lo’ed him weel, that instead o’ bein’ veesible afore their een, he micht hide himsel’ in their verra herts. David Elginbrod

veesion

vision

4 in Alec Forbes of Howglen, Malcolm and The Marquis of Lossie. Forms: veesion (4).

And do ye min' the veesion that the apostle John saw in Pawtmos? Alec Forbes of Howglen

veesit

visit

14 in 4 novels. Forms: veesits (6), veesitin' (2), veesit (2), veesitit (1), veesitation (1), veesitor (1), veesitin (1).

I hae hard o’ creepin’ things ‘at veesits fowk ’at’s no weel Malcolm

vengefu'

vengeful

1 in Malcolm. Forms: vengefu' (1).

She’s an awfu’ vengefu’ cratur, fowk says. Malcolm

ventur

venture

2 in Malcolm and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: ventur (2).

But it’s a some fearsome ventur, an’ may be luikit intill, ye ken. Malcolm

vertue

virtue

2 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: vertue (1), vertues (1).

Weel, that's a vertue. Alec Forbes of Howglen

v'ice

voice

9 in 6 novels. Forms: v'ice (9).

An’ the soon’ o’ his v’ice was aye safter nor the words o’ his mooth. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

vicktooals

victuals

1 in The Marquis of Lossie. Forms: vicktooals (1).

Jist rin ye hame, an’ get some vicktooals thegither, an’ come efter hiz to Portlossie. The Marquis of Lossie

vooed

vowed

2 in Donal Grant and The Marquis of Lossie. Forms: vooed (2).

… she heild her tongue, I jist pu’d the bridle o’ mine, an’ vooed there should be never anither word said by me till ance she … Donal Grant

vouchsaufed

vouchsafed

1 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: vouchsaufed (1).

I'm no sayin' 'at a man's no ane o' the elec' that hasna had that favour vouchsaufed to him Alec Forbes of Howglen

vout

vault

5 in Malcolm. Forms: vouts (3), voutit (1), vout (1).

There’s awkward kin’ o’ holes aboot thae vouts, an’ jist whaur ye think there’s nane. Malcolm

vraith

wreath, snowdrift

2 in David Elginbrod and Robert Falconer. Forms: vraith (2).

She’s i’ the Lord’s han’s, Janet, be she aneath a snaw-vraith. David Elginbrod

vrang

wrong

2 in Malcolm and Robert Falconer. Forms: vrang (2).

But I s’ gar ye lauch o’ the vrang side o’ your mou’ yet, my man. Malcolm

vratch

wretch

9 in 5 novels. Forms: vratch (9).

Ye're a leein' vratch, Willie Macwha, as ye weel ken i' yer ain conscience! Alec Forbes of Howglen

vreet

write

3 in David Elginbrod, Robert Falconer and Sir Gibbie. Forms: vrite (1), vrote (1), vreet (1).

It’s verra kin’ o’ ye to vrite till’s in the verra moment o’ victory David Elginbrod

vricht

wright, carpenter

2 in Salted with Fire. Forms: vricht (2).

MacDonald glosses it: “carpenter”.

Ye’ll see and sen’ a message to the vricht (carpenter) the day? Salted with Fire

vroucht

wrought, worked

5 in Robert Falconer, Sir Gibbie and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: vroucht (5).

or for the life o’ the yoong laird, vroucht to deith wi’ labourers’ wark, an’ syne ‘maist smoored i’ storm? Warlock o’ Glenwarlock
