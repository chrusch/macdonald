Scots Words

Scots Words: Y

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWYZ

yaird

yard

21 in 8 novels. Forms: yaird (8), yairds (6), yerd (5), yerdin' (1), yerd's (1).

MacDonald glosses it: “garden”.

To tice them oot o’ the yaird, laddie, The worms, for my daily food. Sir Gibbie

yallow

yellow

4 in Alec Forbes of Howglen, Malcolm and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: yallow (4).

That wad be jist yallow-faced Willie ower again![3] Malcolm

ye

you

7,484 in all 11 novels. Forms: ye (7,419), 'ye (65). One of the 100 commonest.

but jist say a fair word for the livin' ower the deid, ye ken. Alec Forbes of Howglen

ye'll

you will

392 in all 11 novels. Forms: ye'll (385), 'ye'll (7). One of the 100 commonest.

Ye'll hae your share in bringin' destruction upo' this toon, and a' its belongin's. Alec Forbes of Howglen

yeneps

pence (slang)

1 in Robert Falconer. Forms: yeneps (1).

MacDonald glosses it: “pence”.

… in yer coat-tails, a flatch yenork (half-crown) in yer waistcoat, and yer yeneps (pence) in yer breeches. Robert Falconer

yenork

half-crown (slang)

1 in Robert Falconer. Forms: yenork (1).

MacDonald glosses it: “half-crown”.

… sir, and keep a yellow dump (sovereign) in yer coat-tails, a flatch yenork (half-crown) in yer waistcoat, and yer yeneps (pence) in yer breeches. Robert Falconer

yer

your; you're

1,543 in all 11 novels. Forms: yer (1,540), 'yer (3). One of the 100 commonest.

an' here ye are, on yer ain father's buryin' day, that comes but ance Alec Forbes of Howglen

ye're

you are

553 in all 11 novels. Forms: ye're (540), 'ye're (11), ye'r (2). One of the 100 commonest.

Ye're a leein' vratch, Willie Macwha, as ye weel ken i' yer ain conscience! Alec Forbes of Howglen

yerl

earl

35 in 6 novels. Forms: yerl (31), yerl's (3), yerls (1).

He bauds his lan' frae a higher than the laird, or the yerl himsel'! The Elect Lady

yersel'

yourself

313 in all 11 novels. Forms: yersel' (233), yersel (74), yoursel' (2), yersel's (2), 'yersel (1), yer'sel (1). One of the 100 commonest.

Neist to hearin’ yersel’ tell a tale, I like to hear yer wheel gauin’. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

yersels

yourselves

12 in 7 novels. Forms: yersels (12).

At the same time, wha wud I be greedy for but yersels? Heather and Snow

yerth, yird, yirth

earth

17 in 7 novels. Forms: yird (11), yerth (3), yirth (2), yearth (1).

MacDonald glosses it: “earth”.

To think,’ he went on, ‘o’ a haill kirk o’ cats aneath the yird Heather and Snow

ye'se

you shall

1 in David Elginbrod. Forms: ye'se (1).

But ye’se hae’t as I read it: David Elginbrod

yestreen

yesterday evening

2 in David Elginbrod and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: yestere'en (1), yestreen (1).

for it was only yestreen ‘at the laird himsel’ said to me, ‘at hoo the bairns had never gotten on naething like it wi’ ony … David Elginbrod

yett

gate

14 in 5 novels. Forms: yett (12), yetts (2).

MacDonald glosses it: “gate”; “low gate”.

Ye can easy luik roun the neuk o' the back-yett, an' nobody be a hair the wiser. Alec Forbes of Howglen

yeuk

itch

2 in Sir Gibbie. Forms: yeuks (1), yeuky (1).

MacDonald glosses it: “itches”.

It’s a keerious name till a buik, an’ min’s me o’ naething but whan the lid o’ yer e’e yeuks (itches) Sir Gibbie

ye've

you have

16 in 9 novels. Forms: ye've (16).

On breid ye’re an unco sma’ lairdie, But in hicht ye’ve a kingly gift! Heather and Snow

yirn

yearn

2 in Malcolm and Salted with Fire. Forms: yirnin' (1), yirnin (1).

and langer yet or he see the glint o’ the siller he thoucht he was yirnin by ’t! Salted with Fire

yokit

yoked; set to work

1 in Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: yokit (1).

But, Aggie,” he added, after a pause, “ye ken ye’re no to be oonaiqually yokit. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

yon

that, those (over there)

141 in 10 novels. Forms: yon (117), yon's (23), yon'll (1).

Ye maun min’ the tale, better nor I can tell ‘t ye, concernin’ yon meal-girnel Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

yonner, yon'er

yonder

37 in 8 novels. Forms: yonner (18), yon'er (17), yoner (1), yonner's (1).

We hae been prowlin’ aboot the hoose, but no doon yon’er, my leddy. Donal Grant

yont

beyond

2 in Malcolm and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: yont (2).

the win’ maun come frae yont the stars Malcolm

yoong

young

105 in 8 novels. Forms: yoong (100), yoonger (3), yoongest (2).

Bide till the yoong laird comes back wi’ the news: he winna be lang. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

younker

youngster

1 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: younker (1).

I'll hae nae jaw frae you, younker," said he slowly. Alec Forbes of Howglen

yowie

ewe

3 in Heather and Snow, Salted with Fire and The Elect Lady. Forms: yowie (2), yowies (1).

Are ye no the Lord's clean yowie? The Elect Lady

yowl

howl

2 in Heather and Snow and Malcolm. Forms: yowl (1), yowlt (1).

Ony dog wud yowl gien ye threw a kettle o’ bilin watter ower him! Heather and Snow

yowth

youth

3 in Alec Forbes of Howglen and Robert Falconer. Forms: yowth (3).

'Weel, it's a queer instructor o' yowth, 'at says an' onsays i' the same breith.' Robert Falconer
