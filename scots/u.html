Scots Words: U

ugsome

loathsome, horrible

3 in Alec Forbes of Howglen, Sir Gibbie and Warlock o’ Glenwarlock. Forms: ugsome (3).

MacDonald glosses it: “frightful”.

Ye're to come hame wi' me frae this mou' o' hell and ugsome (frightful) deith. Alec Forbes of Howglen

uilyie

oil

1 in Malcolm. Forms: uilyie (1).

Daur ye say I dinna ken hoo to trim an uilyie lamp wi’ the best blin’ piper that ever cam frae the bare-leggit Heelans? Malcolm

uisgebeatha

whisky

2 in Malcolm. Forms: uisgebeatha (2).

Will you pe taking to him a trop of uisgebeatha? Malcolm

umbrell

umbrella

5 in Sir Gibbie. Forms: umbrell (5).

I want to lay my umbrell in safity,” answered Janet, Sir Gibbie

umquhile

late, deceased; formerly

1 in Robert Falconer. Forms: umquhile (1).

Falconer would have been horrified to see his umquhile modest companion in such society as that into which she was now introduced at times. Robert Falconer

unaccoontable

unaccountable

1 in Malcolm. Forms: unaccoontable (1).

It taks a faimily that hauds on throu’ centeries in a hoose whaur things gether, to mak sic an unaccoontable getherin’ o’ buiks as that. Malcolm

unbun'

unbound

1 in Salted with Fire. Forms: unbun' (1).

… ye, like a sheaf o’ corn ye had jist cuttit and left unbun’! Salted with Fire

unce

ounce

3 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: unce (3).

I was stannin' at the coonter o' his shop waitin' for an unce o' sneeshin' Alec Forbes of Howglen

unceevil

uncivil

1 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: unceevil (1).

He's an unceevil fallow. Alec Forbes of Howglen

unco

strange, unusual; very, extremely

125 in 10 novels. Forms: unco (121), unco' (4).

MacDonald glosses it: “uncouthly”.

I hae an ill hert, I doobt, Cosmo, for I’m unco hard to content. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

undrest

undressed

1 in Donal Grant. Forms: undrest (1).

She lay comfortably enough, but had a suspicion that the place was not over clean, and was glad to find herself not undrest. Donal Grant

un'erstan', unnerstan'

understand

141 in all 11 novels. Forms: un'erstan' (70), unnerstan' (37), un'erstan'in' (10), understan' (8), un'erstude (2), un'erstan's (2), un'erstan't (2), un'erstan (2), unnerstan's (2), unnerstans (1), and 5 more.

I believe I un’erstan’ ye whiles ‘maist as weel as ye du yersel’. Warlock o’ Glenwarlock

un'ertak

undertake

4 in Alec Forbes of Howglen, Malcolm and Salted with Fire. Forms: unnertook (2), unnertak' (1), un'ertak (1).

An’ I canna unnertak’ to manage her my lane. Malcolm

un'er, unner

under

16 in 7 novels. Forms: unner (8), un'er (8).

She's turnin' some frail noo, and I'm unner great obligation to her Sandy, ye ken.' Robert Falconer

ungratefu'

ungrateful

1 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: ungratefu' (1).

… always placed himself at such meetings, "gotten up atween yersel' and that ungratefu' cousin o' mine, Jeames Anderson's lass, wha I hae keepit like ane … Alec Forbes of Howglen

unlikly

unlikely

1 in Robert Falconer. Forms: unlikly (1).

'God's aye doin' unlikly things, grannie,' said Robert, solemnly. Robert Falconer

unlockit

unlocked

1 in Donal Grant. Forms: unlockit (1).

Harper went an’ got it as desired, an’ the gentleman took an’ unlockit it, an’ roon’ he turnt whaur he stood, an’ up he tuik … Donal Grant

unrichteous

unrighteous

1 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: unrichteous (1).

… frae the Bible, whilk was a verra unshuitable place to put the unrichteous mammon intil, and min's me upo' the money-changers i' the temple Alec Forbes of Howglen

unscaumit

unscorched

1 in Salted with Fire. Forms: unscaumit (1).

The Lord kens his ain: he’ll see that they come throuw unscaumit! Salted with Fire

unsuitable

1 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: unshuitable (1).

… that I took the bank-note frae the Bible, whilk was a verra unshuitable place to put the unrichteous mammon intil, and min's me upo' the … Alec Forbes of Howglen

unsoucht

unsought

2 in Robert Falconer. Forms: unsoucht (2).

'Preserve me for ane frae a wife that comes unsoucht,' returned the weaver. Robert Falconer

unweel

unwell

1 in Robert Falconer. Forms: unweel (1).

… day 'at I was awa' last ook, for 'deed I was verra unweel, and bude to keep my bed.' Robert Falconer

unweeting

unwitting

1 in David Elginbrod. Forms: unweeting (1).

… or two of another, and there lie in wait till some passer-by, unweeting of harm, was just opposite their lurking cave David Elginbrod

uphaud

uphold

14 in 7 novels. Forms: uphaud (10), uphaudin' (4).

for mad I s’ uphaud him, an’ fit only to be lockit up Donal Grant

upliftit

uplifted

2 in Alec Forbes of Howglen and Sir Gibbie. Forms: upliftit (2).

it’s a caul’ nicht;’ it’s mysel’ wad hae been sae upliftit wi’ yer kin’ness, ‘at I wad hae gane hame an’ ta’en Sir Gibbie

upmak

3 in Alec Forbes of Howglen. Forms: upmak (2), upmak' (1).

But I'll sing ye a sang, by way o' upmak'. Alec Forbes of Howglen

upo'

upon

896 in all 11 novels. Forms: upo' (848), upo (43), upo's (2), upo't (2), upon's (1). One of the 100 commonest.

The word itsel' oot o' his mou' fa's as deid as chaff upo' clay. Alec Forbes of Howglen

upricht

2 in Alec Forbes of Howglen and David Elginbrod. Forms: upricht (2).

It's a strange thing, wumman, but aye whan a body's tryin' maist to gang upricht he's sure to catch a dreidfu' fa'. Alec Forbes of Howglen

1 in Malcolm. Forms: upstrauchtin' (1).

That may be ‘s it may, but in cam the prence, wi’ a laich boo, an’ a gran’ upstrauchtin’ again Malcolm

useful

Feet’s gey usefu’ kin o’ thing’s to craturs, whether gien them in fours or twas! Heather and Snow
