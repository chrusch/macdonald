Index of Quotations

The verses of the Bible quoted in MacDonald’s books, in the order of the Bible, each with the places that quote it: his sermons first, then his essays, novels and stories, and poems. Click a verse’s number to read it, in the King James Version and, where he quotes the Revised Version instead, in that too. Each title leads to the paragraph where the verse is quoted; a ¶ after it, to another paragraph there that quotes it again. In the novels the words are often a character’s.

How the quotations were found

The verses were found by setting the books word for word beside the King James Version and the Revised Version of 1881–85, which he also used. A verse counts as quoted when six or seven of its words, or nearly all of a short verse, stand in the same order; the novels, whose telling shares more turns of phrase with the Bible’s stories, had to match more closely. Allusions and paraphrases are not counted, and a few stock phrases may be. England’s Antiphon and Hamlet, chiefly other men’s words, are left out.
