Who was George MacDonald?

Born in Huntly, Aberdeenshire, in 1824, MacDonald trained for the Congregational ministry, lost his first pulpit within three years for teaching too large a hope, and spent the rest of his life writing: some fifty books of novels, fairy tales, poems and sermons.

His fantasies, Phantastes, At the Back of the North Wind, the Princess books and Lilith, shaped the imaginations of G. K. Chesterton and C. S. Lewis and opened the way for the modern fantasy that followed them. His sermons, above all the Unspoken Sermons, set out a faith in a God whose love will not let any creature go.
