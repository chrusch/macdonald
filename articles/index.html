Short studies of what MacDonald believed, made mostly of his own words. Every quotation links to the paragraph it comes from in these editions, so it can be read in its place.

  1. Was George MacDonald a Hopeful Universalist?

    November 12, 2026

    No. MacDonald is better called a convinced universalist. He states the final salvation of all as belief, grounded in God’s fatherhood, though he refuses to make it a dogma.

  2. The Goodness of God in George MacDonald

    November 12, 2026

    At the heart of MacDonald’s faith is one conviction: God is good, and good as a father is good. The passages below, from his sermons and his novels, show that goodness in his own words: a Father who is like Jesus, in whom there is nothing deeper than love, who is near to the one in the dark, and who delights in the world he made.
