A Glossary of MacDonald’s Terms

Spirit

Holy Spirit · spirit of the Father

Frequency in the corpus: the Spirit, Holy Spirit, Holy Ghost and Spirit of God in about 140 paragraphs. Most frequent in Unspoken Sermons: “The Higher Faith”, “It Shall Not be Forgiven”, “Abba, Father!” and “The Knowing of the Son”.

In brief. The Spirit is the life of the Father and the Son in a man: the mind of Christ given to him, the spirit of the child toward the Father. MacDonald stresses that the Spirit has a living revelation for each man, which the Bible serves but cannot replace. The Spirit is known by its witness with our spirit, and to shut it out is the one sin that cannot be forgiven while it lasts, since forgiveness can enter only by the Spirit. There is but one spirit, that of truth, and having the same spirit is the only unity.

Shades of meaning

  1. The mind of Christ. “The Son of God is the Teacher of men, giving to them of his Spirit—that Spirit which manifests the deep things of God, being to a man the mind of Christ. The great heresy of the Church of the present day is unbelief in this Spirit” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Higher Faith”). “The Christ in us, is the spirit of the perfect child toward the perfect father” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Creation in Christ”). See child · sonship.

  2. A revelation for each man. “The mass of the Church does not believe that the Spirit has a revelation for every man individually—a revelation as different from the revelation of the Bible, as the food in the moment of passing into living brain and nerve differs from the bread and meat” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Higher Faith”). “If I did not believe in a special inspiration to every man who asks for the holy spirit, the good thing of God, I should have to throw aside the whole tale as an imposture” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Knowing of the Son”). See revelation and individual.

  3. The meaning in the Word. “Even Christ himself is not The Word of God in the deepest sense to a man, until he is this Revelation of God to the man,—until the Spirit that is the meaning in the Word has come to him” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Temptation in the Wilderness”). Those who hold God to his promises “prefer the Word to the Spirit: it is theirs” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Higher Faith”). The letter “should not be throned with power to kill” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Knowing of the Son”). See revelation and opinion · doctrine.

  4. Witnessing with our spirit. “The Spirit of God is the Spirit whose influence is known by its witnessing with our spirit” (Unspoken Sermons, “It Shall Not be Forgiven”). What a man likes best “may be the voice of the Spirit striving with his spirit, not against it” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Higher Faith”). See conscience.

  5. The sin against it. The man who denies truth he sees, “who says that which is good is of Satan, or that which is bad is of God, supposing him to know that it is good or is bad, denies the Spirit, shuts out the Spirit, and therefore cannot be forgiven. For without the Spirit no forgiveness can enter the man to cast out the satan” (Unspoken Sermons, “It Shall Not be Forgiven”). Yet the voice of the Spirit may still be faintly heard, “the still small voice that comes after the tempest and the earthquake” (same). See consuming fire and hell.

  6. The spirit of the Father and the Brother. “Nothing will help but the Spirit proceeding from the Father and the Son, the spirit of the Father and the Brother casting out and revealing” (Unspoken Sermons, “Love Thine Enemy”). The doxology becomes “glory be to the Father and to the Son, and to the spirit of both, the fatherhood of the Father meeting and blending with the sonhood of the Son” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Creation in Christ”). See Father and oneness · harmony.

  7. The one spirit of unity. “If we have not each other’s spirits, we do not belong to each other. There is no unity but having the same spirit. There is but one spirit, that of truth” (Unspoken Sermons, “Abba, Father!”). The “spirit of adoption” Luther renders “the spirit of a child” (same). See truth.

  8. The Spirit that rouses. “Demands unknown before are continually being made upon the Christian: it is the ever fresh rousing and calling, asking and sending of the Spirit that worketh in the children of obedience” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Hardness of the Way”). The spirit-working of the Lord in our hearts “is all in order that we may, as we must, do it ourselves” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Creation in Christ”). See growth and obedience.

Neighbouring words

  • mind of Christ (its content). Sense 1.
  • inspiration (its gift to each). Sense 2.
  • letter, Word, Bible (what it gives meaning to). Sense 3.
  • spirit (of man) (what it witnesses with). Sense 4.
  • truth (its name). Sense 7; “no man can see a true thing to be true but by the Lord, the spirit” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Knowing of the Son”).
  • adoption, childship (its cry). Sense 7; see child · sonship.
  • unpardonable sin (its refusal). Sense 5.

Scripture. 1 Corinthians 2:10–16, the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God … we have the mind of Christ (senses 1 and 2); Romans 8:15–16, the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father. The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit (senses 4 and 7); Luke 11:13, how much more shall your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him? (sense 2); Luke 12:10, unto him that blasphemeth against the Holy Ghost it shall not be forgiven (sense 5); 2 Corinthians 3:6 and 18, the letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life and the Lord the Spirit (RV) (sense 3).
