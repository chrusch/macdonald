A Glossary of MacDonald’s Terms

stupidity

stupid · dullness

Frequency in the non-fiction: stupid and stupidity 68. The central source is the sermon “The Cause of Spiritual Stupidity”, on Mark 8:14–21.

In brief. Not want of intelligence but want of spiritual perception: the dullness that cannot take in what the Lord means. MacDonald finds its cause in care, distrust and the dominion of things, and never in the intellect. The disciples did not understand the warning about leaven because their minds were full of the day’s bread. Stupidity is overcome by trust and by obedience, since the word of the Master unfolds itself only to the man trying to live it. Its worst form cannot know itself.

Shades of meaning

  1. The disciples’ failure. The sermon’s text is the Lord’s reproach, How is it that ye do not understand? MacDonald answers it: “their minds were so full of care about the day’s bread, that they could not think with simplicity about anything else; the mere mention of leaven threw them floundering afresh in the bog of their unbelief” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Cause of Spiritual Stupidity”). Once “their fear was taught to look up, that moment they began to see what the former words of the Lord must have meant” (same). See understand.

  2. Care the cause. “The care that is filling your mind at this moment, or but waiting till you lay the book aside to leap upon you—that need which is no need, is a demon sucking at the spring of your life” (same). Care comes from distrust: “Distrust is atheism, and the barrier to all growth. Lord, we do not understand thee, because we do not trust thy Father” (same). See fear and faith.

  3. Things the cause. Having and wanting dull alike: “The money the one has, the money the other would have, is in each the cause of an eternal stupidity” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Hardness of the Way”). See having · possession, sense 7.

  4. Cured by living. Understanding is a fruit of obedience, not of cleverness: “It is to the man who is trying to live, to the man who is obedient to the word of the Master, that the word of the Master unfolds itself” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Cause of Spiritual Stupidity”). The Lord’s words yield “no satisfactory meaning to the mere intellect” (same), “For life, that is, action, is alone the human condition into which the light of the Living can penetrate; life alone can assimilate life, can change food into growth” (same). See obedience and life.

  5. Reading the figures low. A dull mind takes the Lord’s symbols as saying less than they say: “It is the low stupidity of an unspiritual nature that would interpret the Lord’s meaning as less than his symbols” (Unspoken Sermons, “Self-Denial”). See symbol.

  6. Stupidity that cannot know itself. The refusal to hope is a dullness of the same kind: “The stupid, self-satisfied soul, which cannot know its own stupidity, and will not trouble itself either to understand or to imagine” (The Hope of the Gospel, “The Hope of the Universe”) prefers “a dull scheme of things” (same). Familiarity breeds it too: “men have grown stupid to the wonder inherent in it” (A Dish of Orts, “A Sketch of Individual Development”). See hope and wonder.

  7. Dull times, and what to do in them. Stupidity is also a passing state of the believer, not to be explained away as merely bodily: “let us arise and live—arise even in the darkest moments of spiritual stupidity, when hope itself sees nothing to hope for” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Creation in Christ”). The counsel is to “go at once to the Life” (same).

Neighbouring words

  • understand (what it lacks). Sense 1; see understand.
  • care, anxiety (its chief cause). Sense 2. MacDonald calls care “the mosquitoes of care” that drown “the voice of the Eternal” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Cause of Spiritual Stupidity”).
  • distrust, unbelief (its root). Sense 2.
  • things, money (its occasion). Sense 3.
  • obedience, life (its cure). Sense 4.
  • see, sight (its opposite). See see · vision, sense 7.
  • dullness, deadness (its state). Senses 6 and 7.

Scripture. Mark 8:14–21, How is it that ye do not understand?, the text of “The Cause of Spiritual Stupidity”; Matthew 6:34, Take therefore no thought for the morrow, behind sense 2; John 7:17, If any man will do his will, he shall know of the doctrine (sense 4); Mark 8:18, Having eyes, see ye not? and having ears, hear ye not?
