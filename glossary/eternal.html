A Glossary of MacDonald’s Terms

eternal

eternal life · eternity

Frequency in the non-fiction: eternal and eternally 245; eternity 36; everlasting 14. The main sources are “Life”, “The Way” and “The Hardness of the Way”.

In brief. For MacDonald eternal names a kind of life, not a length of time. Eternal life is knowing God, oneness with him, life “come awake”; it is God’s own life shared by children who do his will. Mere endless existence without it would be “worthless as hell”. He therefore cares little for the certainty of eternal life and much for the thing itself, and he speaks of eternal facts, eternal hunger and eternal death as qualities of being. God himself is the Eternal, and Jesus came to give men God, who is eternal life.

Shades of meaning

  1. Knowing God. “The knowledge of the living God is eternal life” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Hardness of the Way”). To the rich young man’s question the answer lies here: “to love God with all our heart, and soul, and strength, and mind, is to know God, and to know him is eternal life; that is the end of the whole saving matter” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Way”). See know.

  2. Not mere continuance. “If we do the will of God, eternal life is ours—no mere continuity of existence, for that in itself is worthless as hell, but a being that is one with the essential Life” (Unspoken Sermons, “Life”). “They think they can do without eternal life, if only they may live for ever! Those who know what eternal life means count it the one terror to have to live on without it” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Hardness of the Way”). See life and hell.

  3. Life come awake. Lower forms of life are on the way, but “true life knows and rules itself; the eternal life is life come awake” (Unspoken Sermons, “Life”). See consciousness.

  4. Oneness with God. “This life, this eternal life, consists for man in absolute oneness with God and all divine modes of being, oneness with every phase of right and harmony” (same). See oneness · harmony.

  5. Perfection. What the rich young man lacked was what the law could not give: “Perfection, the perfection of the Father, is eternal life” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Way”). He “desired eternal life—of which there was no word in the law” (same). See law.

  6. The thing, not the assurance. “It is not assurance I desire, but the thing itself; not the certainty of eternal life, but eternal life” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Hardness of the Way”). See salvation, sense 4.

  7. God is eternal life. “Only God the gift can turn that sorrow into essential joy: Jesus came to give them God, who is eternal life” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Cause of Spiritual Stupidity”). MacDonald addresses God as “thou, the eternal life” (The Hope of the Gospel, “The Right Hand and the Left”). “Not for a moment can we live our true life without the eternal life present in and with our spirits” (The Hope of the Gospel, “The Remission of Sins”). See Father.

  8. Eternal as a quality. A truth once seen is eternal in its effect: “To see one divine fact is to stand face to face with essential eternal life” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Consuming Fire”). Time and place are worth nothing without it: “no spot in space, no hour in eternity is worth anything to one who remains such as he is” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Inheritance”). Its opposite is “eternal death” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Last Farthing”), the end of any way of escaping righteousness. See death.

Neighbouring words

  • life, essential life (what it qualifies). Senses 2 to 4; see life.
  • know, knowledge (what it is). Sense 1.
  • oneness (its content). Sense 4.
  • perfection (its measure). Sense 5.
  • immortality, continuity, live for ever (what it is not). Sense 2.
  • eternal death (its opposite). Sense 8.
  • the Eternal (a name of God). Sense 7; MacDonald speaks of the will of “the eternal, the perfect Father” (The Hope of the Gospel, “The Yoke of Jesus”).

Scripture. John 17:3, this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent (sense 1); Mark 10:17, what shall I do that I may inherit eternal life?, the text of “The Way”; 1 John 3:15, no murderer hath eternal life abiding in him; 1 John 5:11, God hath given to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son (sense 7).
