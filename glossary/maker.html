A Glossary of MacDonald’s Terms

Maker

creation · creature · create

Frequency in the non-fiction: Maker 16 (and maker in lower case); create and its forms, creation, creator, creature 516. The key passages are in “Life”, “Abba, Father!”, “The Creation in Christ” and the essay on the imagination.

In brief. God’s making of beings that are not himself yet like himself, and that can will. For MacDonald creation flows from love: “The being of God is love, therefore creation” (Unspoken Sermons, “Life”). It is the costliest labour of God, begun far back and not yet finished, since the creature is completed only by its own consent. Maker is a true name of God, but not a sufficient one: a God who is only Maker and not Father would make machines, not children. Man’s art may be called creation only in a lesser sense.

Shades of meaning

  1. Creation from love. “God is life, and the will-source of life. In the outflowing of that life, I know him; and when I am told that he is love, I see that if he were not love he would not, could not create” (Unspoken Sermons, “Life”). Hence “The being of God is love, therefore creation. I imagine that from all eternity he has been creating” (same). See love and life.

  2. The labour of a God. The Son’s existence is “an easy outgoing of the Father’s nature; while to make other beings—beings like us, I imagine the labour of a God, an eternal labour” (same). The difficulty is to make a creature capable of will, which requires “this separation from himself such that will in the creature shall be possible” (same). See will.

  3. Separation for the sake of oneness. Creation divides in order to unite: “the final end of the separation is not individuality; that is but a means to it; the final end is oneness—an impossibility without it” (same).

  4. Beyond understanding. “self-existence and creation no man will ever understand” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Creation in Christ”). God creates “by his essential being alone, that by which he is” (Unspoken Sermons, “Self-Denial”). See being, sense 2.

  5. Maker but also Father. The bare title frightens the child who fears it is the only one: “alas, if he be only a maker and not a father! Then am I only a machine, and not a child—not a man!” (Unspoken Sermons, “Abba, Father!”). The answer to the objection “But creation is not fatherhood” (same) is “Creation in the image of God, is” (same). See Father.

  6. The creature completed by its consent. Creation is still going on in each man, who must take part in it: his obedient action is “his response to his maker’s design, his individual part in the creation of himself” (Unspoken Sermons, “Love Thy Neighbour”). God keeps the man’s idea “through the long process of creation that went to realize the idea” (Unspoken Sermons, “The New Name”). See being, sense 4.

  7. The groaning creation. Following Romans 8, the whole creation works toward the sons of God: “‘More life!’ is the unconscious prayer of all creation, groaning and travailing for the redemption of its lord, the son who is not yet a son” (Unspoken Sermons, “Life”). “Beyond its own will or knowledge, the whole creation works for the development of the children of God into the sons of God” (Unspoken Sermons, “Abba, Father!”).

  8. Man’s creation. The imagination has “been called the creative faculty, and its exercise creation. Poet means maker” (A Dish of Orts, “The Imagination: Its Functions and its Culture”). But “in no primary sense is this faculty creative” (same); we might call it creative “did we not see a something in God for which we would humbly keep our mighty word” (same). See imagination.

Neighbouring words

  • Father (the fuller name). Sense 5; see Father.
  • love, life (its source). Sense 1.
  • will (its highest product). Sense 2; see will.
  • separation, individuality, oneness (its process and end). Sense 3.
  • idea, new name (what it realizes). Sense 6; see being, sense 6.
  • redemption, new creation (its continuation). “it takes a long time to finish the new creation of this redemption” (Unspoken Sermons, “Abba, Father!”).
  • imagination, poet, maker (its human likeness). Sense 8.

Scripture. Genesis 1:27, So God created man in his own image, behind sense 5; Romans 8:19–22, the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God … the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now (sense 7); John 1:3, All things were made by him, the text of “The Creation in Christ”.
