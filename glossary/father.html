A Glossary of MacDonald’s Terms

Father

fatherhood

Frequency in the non-fiction: father 1,346; fatherhood 23.

In brief. God’s own name, and for MacDonald the truest one: God is the Father, first of the eternal Son and then of every man. Fatherhood is not one attribute among others, or an office God holds beside that of judge; it is what he is, and every other word about God must be read through it. The human father is its copy, not its model.

Shades of meaning

  1. The central truth, and its refusal the central wrong. “The refusal to look up to God as our Father is the one central wrong in the whole human affair; the inability, the one central misery” (Unspoken Sermons, “Abba, Father!”). The sentence goes on to promise that whatever clears the way to “the recognition of the Father, will more or less undermine every difficulty in life” (same paragraph, “Abba, Father!”).

  2. Father of the Son. The eternal relation inside God is the pattern of all fatherhood: “from all eternity the Father has had the Son, and the never-begun existence of that Son I imagine an easy outgoing of the Father’s nature” (Unspoken Sermons, “Life”). The Son’s whole life answers it: “Because his father is his father, therefore he will be his child” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Creation in Christ”).

  3. Father of every man, by creation in his image. MacDonald holds that every man is God’s child before he chooses to be, and grounds it in the image: “Creation in the image of God, is” fatherhood, and “nothing can make it that I am not the child of God” (Unspoken Sermons, “Abba, Father!”). Even guilt witnesses to it: “I cling fast to my blame: it is the seal of my childhood” (same paragraph, “Abba, Father!”).

  4. More than Maker. Maker names the bare fact of origin; Father names a relation that lasts and loves. Without immortality, he says, God “could only be our Maker, not our Father, our Origin” (Unspoken Sermons, “The New Name”).

  5. Father, not magistrate. The word is MacDonald’s main weapon against penal theology, which divides God into offices: it “represents him, for instance, as having to do that as a magistrate which as a father he would not do! The love of the father makes him desire to be unjust as a magistrate!” (Unspoken Sermons, “Justice”). Likewise, “he who trusts in the atonement instead of in the father of Jesus Christ, fills his fancy with the chimeras of a vulgar legalism” (The Hope of the Gospel, “God’s Family”).

  6. The Father of Jesus, against the God of the theologians. The phrase the father of Jesus Christ identifies which God is meant: “I will not have the God of the scribes and the pharisees whether Jewish or Christian, protestant, Roman, or Greek, but thy father, O Christ! He is my God” (Unspoken Sermons, “Justice”).

  7. Father and mother. The word includes what is motherly: God is “simply and altogether our friend, our father—our more than friend, father, and mother—our infinite love-perfect God” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Child in the Midst”).

  8. The source of human fatherhood. Human fatherhood is derived from God’s, not projected onto him: “The Fatherhood of God created the fatherhood in man; God’s love man’s love” (Miracles, “Miracles Granted to the Prayer of Friends”).

  9. A father who gives himself. Fatherhood is devotion, not authority: “Let us not forget that the devotion of the Son could never have been but for the devotion of the Father, who never seeks his own glory one atom more than does the Son” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Creation in Christ”).

In the fiction and poetry. The novels turn sense 9 into the labour of a parent. In Robert Falconer the boy’s discovery is “that in the giving of that help the very fatherhood of the Father finds its one gladsome labour” (Robert Falconer, ch. XII). In the Diary of an Old Soul the address to the Father becomes a claim: “I am lost before thee, Father! yet I will / Claim of thee my birthright ineffable” (Diary of an Old Soul, June).

Neighbouring words

  • child, son, sonship (correlates). Every use of Father implies the child; sonship is the answer to fatherhood (sense 2). See son.
  • Maker, creator (lesser names). Origin without the lasting relation (sense 4).
  • magistrate, judge, king (rival images). Rejected where they divide God from his fatherhood (sense 5); accepted where they serve it.
  • elder brother (mediator). The Son shows the younger children the Father: “He lets him see how he delights in his father, and lets him know that God is his father too” (The Hope of the Gospel, “The Yoke of Jesus”).
  • love (substance). “The Fatherhood and the Sonship are one, save that the Fatherhood looks down lovingly, and the Sonship looks up lovingly. Love is all” (Unspoken Sermons, “The Child in the Midst”).
  • image (ground). The child of God is so because made in the image (sense 3).
  • home, house, family (figures). Heaven as the Father’s house; mankind as “God’s Family”, the title of a chapter of The Hope of the Gospel.

Scripture. Romans 8:15 and Galatians 4:6, Abba, Father (KJV), give the title of the sermon behind senses 1 and 3; Luke 15, the prodigal son, I will arise and go to my father, which MacDonald quotes in “Life” for the choice by which a creature turns to its source.
