Index of Quotations

The verses of the Bible quoted in MacDonald’s sermons, in the order of the Bible, each with the sermons that quote it. Each title leads to the paragraph where the verse is quoted; a ¶ after it, to another paragraph that quotes it again.

The verses were found by setting the sermons word for word beside the King James Version and the Revised Version of 1881–85, which he also used. A verse counts as quoted when six or seven of its words, or nearly all of a short verse, stand in a sermon in the same order. Allusions and paraphrases are not counted, and a few stock phrases may be that he used without thinking of the verse. The texts the sermons are preached on are in the Index of Texts.
