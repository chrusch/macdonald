About this digital edition

How the text was made, and the printer's errors corrected

The text is Project Gutenberg’s transcription (ebook #5773), from the public-domain printed edition, made by volunteers.

For this site it was converted to Markdown: Project Gutenberg’s header and licence, the printed page numbers, and the printed table of contents were removed, and the headings were regularised so the book divides into chapters. The words of the text are as Project Gutenberg gives them.

The eight plates come from a scan of the Strahan edition of about 1868 at the Internet Archive (annalsofquietnei00macduoft), with the lines printed under them as their captions. They are unsigned; Robert Barnes illustrated the novel when it ran in the Sunday Magazine in 1865–66.
