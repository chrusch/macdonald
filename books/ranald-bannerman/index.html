About this digital edition

How the text was made, and the printer's errors corrected

The text is Project Gutenberg’s transcription (ebook #9301), from the public-domain printed edition, made by volunteers.

For this site it was converted to Markdown: Project Gutenberg’s header and licence, the printed page numbers, and the printed table of contents were removed, and the headings were regularised so the book divides into chapters. The words of the text are as Project Gutenberg gives them.

Arthur Hughes’s illustrations come from a scan of the 1874 Lippincott edition at the Internet Archive (ranaldbannermans00macd), which prints the drawings he made for the first edition of 1871. (Project Gutenberg’s text comes from a later Blackie edition, whose coloured plates by A. V. Wheelhouse are left out.)
