About this digital edition

How the text was made, and the printer's errors corrected

The text is Project Gutenberg’s transcription (ebook #56176), from the public-domain printed edition, made by volunteers.

For this site it was converted to Markdown: Project Gutenberg’s header and licence, the printed page numbers, and the printed table of contents were removed, and the headings were regularised so the book divides into chapters. The words of the text are as Project Gutenberg gives them.

The six pictures come from scans at the Internet Archive of the edition this text follows (New York: Routledge, 1883), as reissued by McKay of Philadelphia in 1911 (guildcourtlondon00macd_0), and for the one plate that reissue leaves out, of the 1883 printing itself (guildcourtlondon00macduoft). They are unsigned. G. J. Pinwell’s drawings for the novel’s serialisation in Good Words (1867) are a different set.
