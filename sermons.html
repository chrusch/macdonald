His Sermons

MacDonald left his pulpit at Arundel before he had held it three years, but he never stopped preaching. He preached where he was asked, and above all on paper: in the three series of Unspoken Sermons and in The Hope of the Gospel, and through the preachers of his novels, clergy and lay, who say what he believed. The papers of The Miracles of Our Lord are not sermons, but they read the Gospels as closely as any sermon does, and they are here too.

All of them are gathered here, each with the words of the Bible it was preached on. To search the sermons alone, use the box below, or choose “Sermons” on the search page.

Unspoken Sermons

First Series1867

  1. The Child in the Midst

    …And he took a child, and set him in the midst of them: and when he had taken him in his arms, he said unto them, Whosoever shall receive one of such children in my name, receiveth me; and whosoever shall receive me, receiveth not me, but him that sent me. Mark 9:33–37

  2. The Consuming Fire

    Our God is a consuming fire. Hebrews 12:29

  3. The Higher Faith

    Jesus saith unto him, Thomas, because thou hast seen me, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed. John 20:29

  4. It Shall Not be Forgiven

    And whosoever shall speak a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him: but unto him that blasphemeth against the Holy Ghost, it shall not be forgiven. Luke 12:10

  5. The New Name

    To him that overcometh, I will give a white stone, and in the stone a new name written, which no man knoweth saving he that receiveth it. Revelation 2:17

  6. The Heart with the Treasure

    Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth… But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven… For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also. Matthew 6:19–21

  7. The Temptation in the Wilderness

    Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness, to be tempted of the devil… Matthew 4:1–11

  8. The Eloi

    My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? Matthew 27:46

  9. The Hands of the Father

    Father, into thy hand I commend my spirit. Luke 23:46

  10. Love Thy Neighbour

    Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself. Matthew 22:39

  11. Love Thine Enemy

    Ye have heard that it hath been said, Thou shalt love thy neighbour, and hate thine enemy; but I say unto you, Love your enemies… Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect. Matthew 5:43–48

  12. The God of the Living

    He is not a God of the dead, but of the living: for all live unto him. Luke 20:38

Second Series1885

  1. The Way

    If thou wouldest be perfect. Matthew 19:21

  2. The Hardness of the Way

    Children, how hard is it! Mark 10:24

  3. The Cause of Spiritual Stupidity

    How is it that ye do not understand? Mark 8:21

  4. The Word of Jesus on Prayer

    They ought always to pray. Luke 18:1

  5. Man's Difficulty Concerning Prayer

    —and not to faint. Luke 18:1

  6. The Last Farthing

    Verily I say unto thee, thou shalt by no means come out thence, till thou have paid the last farthing. Matthew 5:26

  7. Abba, Father!

    —the spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father. Romans 8:15

  8. Life

    I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly. John 10:10

  9. The Fear of God

    And when I saw him, I fell at his feet as one dead. And he laid his right hand upon me, saying, Fear not; I am the first and the last and the Living one. Revelation 1:17–18

  10. The Voice of Job

    O that thou wouldest hide me in the grave, that thou wouldest keep me secret, until thy wrath be past… If a man die, shall he live again?… Job 14:13–15

  11. Self-Denial

    And he said unto all, If any man would come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow me… Luke 9:23–24

  12. The Truth in Jesus

    But ye did not so learn Christ; if so be that ye heard him, and were taught in him, even as truth is in Jesus: that ye put away, as concerning your former manner of life, the old man, which waxeth corrupt after the lusts of deceit. Ephesians 4:20–22

Third Series1889

  1. The Creation in Christ

    All things were made by him, and without him was not anything made that was made. In him was life, and the life was the light of men. John 1:3–4

  2. The Knowing of the Son

    Ye have neither heard his voice at any time, nor seen his shape. And ye have not his word abiding in you; for whom he hath sent, him ye believe not. John 5:37–38

  3. The Mirrors of the Lord

    But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image from glory to glory, even as by the spirit of the Lord. 2 Corinthians 3:18

  4. The Truth

    I am the truth. John 14:6

  5. Freedom

    The Truth shall make you free.… If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed. John 8:32, 34–36

  6. Kingship

    Art thou a king then? Jesus answered, Thou sayest that I am a king! To this end was I born, and for this cause came I into the world, that I should bear witness unto the truth… John 18:37

  7. Justice

    Also unto thee, O Lord, belongeth mercy; for thou renderest to every man according to his work. Psalm 62:12

  8. Light

    This then is the message which we have heard of him, and declare unto you, that God is light, and in him is no darkness at all. 1 John 1:5; John 3:19

  9. The Displeasure of Jesus

    When Jesus therefore saw her weeping, and the Jews also weeping which came with her, he groaned in the spirit, and was troubled. John 11:33

  10. Righteousness

    —that I may win Christ, and be found in him, not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith. Philippians 3:8–9

  11. The Final Unmasking

    For there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed; and hid, that shall not be known. Matthew 10:26; Luke 12:2

  12. The Inheritance

    Giving thanks unto the Father, which hath made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light. Colossians 1:12

The Miracles of Our Lord

1870

Papers, as he calls them, rather than sermons, but reading the Gospel stories as closely as any sermon.

  1. I. Introduction
  2. II. The Beginning of Miracles

    The water made wine at Cana · John 2:1–11

  3. III. The Cure of Simon's Wife's Mother

    Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; Luke 4:38–39

  4. IV. Miracles of Healing Unsolicited

    The woman bowed together, the withered hand, the man at the pool of Bethesda, the man born blind, the man with the dropsy

  5. V. Miracles of Healing Solicited by the Sufferers

    The woman who touched his garment, the lepers, and the blind men, Bartimaeus among them

  6. VI. Miracles Granted to the Prayer of Friends

    The nobleman’s son, the Greek woman’s daughter, the centurion’s servant, the man borne of four, a deaf man, a blind man

  7. VII. The Casting Out of Devils

    The man in the synagogue, the blind and dumb man, the man among the tombs, the boy the disciples could not heal

  8. VIII. The Raising of the Dead

    The widow’s son at Nain, Jairus’s daughter, and Lazarus

  9. IX. The Government of Nature

    The draughts of fishes, the feeding of the thousands, the walking on the sea, the stilling of the storm, the coin in the fish’s mouth

  10. X. Miracles of Destruction

    The barren fig tree, and the men who fell back in the garden

  11. XI. The Resurrection
  12. XII. The Transfiguration

    Matthew 17:1–9; Mark 9:2–10; Luke 9:28–36

The Hope of the Gospel

1892

  1. Salvation from Sin

    —and thou shalt call his name Jesus; for he shall save his people from their sins. Matthew 1:21

  2. The Remission of Sins

    John did baptize in the wilderness, and preach the baptism of repentance for the remission of sins. Mark 1:4

  3. Jesus in the World

    Son, why hast thou thus dealt with us? behold, thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing. And he said unto them, How is it that ye sought me? wist ye not that I must be about my father’s business?… Luke 2:48–50

  4. Jesus and his Fellow Townsmen

    …The spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor… And he began to say unto them, This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears. Luke 4:16–21

  5. The Heirs of Heaven and Earth

    Blessed are the poor in spirit; for theirs is the kingdom of heaven… Blessed are the meek; for they shall inherit the earth. Matthew 5:3, 5

  6. Sorrow the Pledge of Joy

    Blessed are they that mourn, for they shall be comforted. Matthew 5:4

  7. God's Family

    Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God… Blessed are the peace-makers, for they shall be called the children of God. Matthew 5:8, 6, 9

  8. The Reward of Obedience

    Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy. Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness’ sake, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven… Matthew 5:7, 10–12

  9. The Yoke of Jesus

    I thank thee, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because thou hast hid these things from the wise and prudent, and hast revealed them unto babes… Matthew 11:25–30; Luke 10:21–22

  10. The Salt and the Light of the World

    Ye are the salt of the earth… Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill, cannot be hid… Matthew 5:13–16

  11. The Right Hand and the Left

    Take heed that ye do not your righteousness before men to be seen of them… But when thou doest alms, let not thy left hand know what thy right hand doeth… Matthew 6:1–4

  12. The Hope of the Universe

    For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God. Romans 8:19

From A Dish of Orts

1893

  1. A Sermon

    Read in the Unitarian chapel, Essex Street, London, 1879

    Let us therefore, as many as be perfect, be thus minded; and if in anything ye be otherwise minded, God shall reveal even this unto you. Nevertheless, whereto we have already attained, let us walk by that same. Philippians 3:15–16

  2. True Christian Ministering

    A spoken sermon

    …whosoever will be great among you, let him be your minister; and whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant: even as the Son of Man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many. Matthew 20:25–28
